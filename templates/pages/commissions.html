Please read through the information below before submitting a request. This helps make sure your idea, timeline, and commission type are a good fit.

Important Information

-------------------------------

This is an open commission form, which means you may submit a request at any time. However, I only accept a limited number of commissions at once.

If your commission is selected, I will contact you through one of your preferred contact methods. If you are unable to move forward at that time, please let me know if you would like your request removed or placed back on the waitlist.

If you do not receive a message from me, it means I have not reached your submission yet or I am unable to accept the commission at this time.

★★★

About the Form

-------------------------------

This form is mainly for illustration commission requests. Deadlines may be included, but they are not recommended unless absolutely needed.

If you need to make changes after submitting your request, please contact me by email. Multiple submissions are allowed if they are for different illustration ideas.

★★★

Prices & Payment

-------------------------------

Personal Commissions $200 USD
Commercial Commissions $450 USD
Extra Details $50–$100 USD

Payment is accepted through PayPal. Before the commission begins, payment must be made in full. Partial payments and split payments are not accepted.
