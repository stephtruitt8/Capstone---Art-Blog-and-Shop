Important Information! Please read before submitting a commission request.

This is a open commission form.

This form will remain available for anyone who would like to submit a commission request at any time.

However, I will only be accepting a limited number of commissions at a time, so please be patient if you submit a request and do not receive an immediate response.

If I choose to accept your commission, I will contact you through one of your preferred contact methods. If I reach out and you are unable to move forward with the commission at that time, please let me know whether you would like your submission removed or placed back on the waitlist.

If you do not receive a message from me, it means I either have not gotten to your submission yet or I am unable to take on your commission at this time.

- MORE INFO FORM -

This commission form is primarily for illustration requests.

Deadlines may be included in your submission, but they are not recommended. If you need to make changes to your submission after sending it, please contact me by email.

Multiple submissions for different illustration ideas are allowed.

- PRICE AND PAYMENT METHOD -

Payment Method: PayPal

Personal Commissions: $200 USD

Commercial Commissions: $450 USD

Additional characters, detailed backgrounds, or complex requests may include extra charges ranging from $50–$100 USD.

Before the commission begins, payment must be made in full. Partial payments and split payments are not accepted.

- THE DO NOTS! -

Please do not submit multiple copies of the same commission request if you have not heard back from me.

Please do not submit commissions with tight deadlines. Since this form is open and submissions are selected based on availability, I may not be able to choose or complete certain commissions within a short timeframe.

Please do not submit large-scale projects through this form. For larger projects or special inquiries, please contact me directly by email.

For questions, changes, or special inquiries, please email me here: toxiotrabarts2026@gmail.com
